========================================================================
                  CAMPUS RESIDENCE & HOSTEL CODE OF CONDUCT
========================================================================

1. CURFEW TIMINGS
   - The main hostel gates will be locked strictly at 10:00 PM daily.
   - Residents returning late must provide a signed permission slip 
     from their Department Head or Warden.

2. VISITOR POLICY
   - Guests are only permitted in the main lobby/lounge area.
   - No overnight guests are allowed in dorm rooms under any circumstances.
   - Visitor hours: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

3. ROOM MAINTENANCE & SAFETY
   - Residents are responsible for the cleanliness of their own rooms.
   - High-wattage electrical appliances (heaters, hot plates, induction 
     cookers) are strictly prohibited due to fire safety hazards.
   - Quiet hours start at 11:00 PM to ensure an environment conducive 
     to study.

Failure to comply with these rules will result in disciplinary action 
or eviction from university housing.
========================================================================
